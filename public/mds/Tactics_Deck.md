# Tactics Deck – Battle Tactic / Command Cards

## The Ignax Dais (Battle Tactic/Command)
You complete this battle tactic if you control the Ignax objective at the end of your turn.
Command – Forward to Victory (Reaction – You declared a Charge ability): You can re-roll the charge roll.

## War of Attrition (Battle Tactic/Command)
You complete this battle tactic at the end of turn if any enemy units were destroyed this turn and more enemy units than friendly units were destroyed this turn.
Command – Stand Guard (Enemy Hero Phase): Pick a friendly unit to use this ability. That unit has Strike-First this turn.

## Take Without Warning (Battle Tactic/Command)
You complete this objective at the end of your turn if you gain control of an objective that was controlled by your opponent at the start of the turn and none of the units contesting that objective used a fight ability this turn.
Command – Fight to the Last (Reaction – Opponent declared an attack ability): That unit has Ward (5+) this phase.

## The Behemat Dias (Battle Tactic/Command)
You complete this battle tactic if you control the Behemat objective at the end of your turn.
Command – Scroll of Arcane Bolt (Any Hero Phase): Pick a friendly hero to use this ability, pick a visible enemy unit within 12" of them, then roll a dice. On a 2+, inflict D3 Mortal Damage on that enemy unit.

## The Dracothion Dais (Battle Tactic/Command)
You complete this battle tactic if you control the Dracothian objective at the end of your turn.
Command – Go to Ground (Reaction – Opponent declared a shoot ability): Until the end of the phase, only unmodified hot rolls of 6 hit for attacks that target that unit. However, that unit has strike last this turn.

## Take the Flanks (Battle Tactic/Command)
You complete this battle tactic at the end of your turn if there are any friendly units within 3" of each short battlefield edge.
Command – Redeploy (Enemy Movement Phase): Pick a friendly unit that is not in combat to use this ability. That unit can move up to D6". That unit cannot move into combat during any part of that move.

## Raid (Battle Tactic/Command)
You complete this battle tactic at the end of your turn if any friendly units are wholly within enemy territory and not in combat.
Command – Steel Defense (Reaction – Opponent declared an attack ability): Ignore the rend characteristic of attacks that target that unit this phase.

## Hold Ground (Battle Tactic/Command)
You complete this battle tactic at the end of your turn if there are more friendly models contesting the large terrain feature in your territory than there are enemy models contesting it.
Command – Rise to the Challenge (Any Combat Phase): Pick a friendly hero to use this ability. Heal (D6) that hero.

## Raze (Battle Tactic/Command)
You complete this battle tactic at the end of your turn if there are any friendly units within 3" of the long battlefield edge in enemy territory.
Command – Inspiring Presence (Any Hero Phase): Pick a friendly hero to use this ability. Roll a dice. For the rest of the turn, add the number rolled to the control score of that hero.

## Attack on Two Fronts (Battle Tactic/Command)
You complete this battle tactic at the end of your turn if you control 2 objectives that were controlled by your opponent at the start of the turn.
Command – Call Reinforcements (Any Movement Phase): Pick a friendly infantry or cavalry unit with five or more models that has been destroyed and not already replaced; set up a replacement unit consisting of D3 models from that unit anywhere on the battlefield more than 6" from all enemy units.

## Cut Off the Head (Battle Tactic/Command)
You complete this battle tactic at the end of your turn if: the enemy general was slain this turn or if the enemy general was slain in an earlier turn AND any enemy units were destroyed this turn.
Command – Counter Charge (Enemy Charge Phase): Pick a friendly unit that is not in combat to use this ability. That unit can use a charge ability as if it were your charge phase.

## Do Not Waver (Battle Tactic/Command)
You complete this battle tactic at the end of your turn if any friendly units used a fight ability this turn and no friendly units were destroyed this turn.
Command – Fall Back and Rally (Reaction – You declared a retreat ability): No mortal damage is inflicted on that unit by the retreat ability. In addition, after the ability is resolved, roll one dice for each slain model from that unit. For each 5+, you can return 1 slain model to that unit.
