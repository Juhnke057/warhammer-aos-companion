Place your PDF files in this folder with EXACTLY these filenames:

RULEBOOKS
---------
core-rules.pdf              → AoS Core Rules & Spearhead Rules booklet

FACTION RULES
-------------
stormcast-spearhead.pdf     → Stormcast Eternals Spearhead rules
skaven-spearhead.pdf        → Skaven Spearhead rules
fec-spearhead.pdf           → Flesh-eater Courts Spearhead rules

CARDS & REFERENCE
-----------------
spearhead-reference.pdf     → Spearhead quick reference card
fire-jade-deck.pdf          → Fire and Jade deck (twist + battle tactic cards)

These filenames are used by the app to open the correct PDF when
you tap "Open Rulebook" from within the app.
